# THERE NO WHALE

Primary brand: **THERE NO WHALE**. Author signature: **Mihkihtah**. Do not use a real name in public-facing graphics or copy.

The identity combines oversized experimental sans serif lettering, vivid orange, pool blue, near black, warm ivory, and tactile material studies. The hero pairs retro technology embedded in limestone with a macro citrus texture. The supporting triptych explores chrome, citrus and stone. Meadow imagery is no longer part of the active profile.
